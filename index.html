HOW TO PLAY

Win by breaking the enemy Nexus

Both Nexuses start at 30 Vitality. Vessels do not win the game on their own — they exist to clear a path so one of them can strike the enemy Nexus from its home row.

  1. The objective

    Reduce the opposing Nexus from 30 Vitality to 0. Your Nexus is the marker under your side of the board.

  2. Vessels

    A Vessel is a unit with Attack, Guard, movement and range. Guard is persistent damage — it does not heal back at end of turn, so trades matter.

  3. Where you summon

    Your two home rows are your deployment zone, tinted on the board. Select a Vessel card and every legal tile lights up; summoned Vessels take the four front-row zones above your hand. You may summon one Vessel per turn and hold at most four.

  4. Actions and Relics

    Actions resolve an effect once and go to the discard pile. Relics are persistent and occupy one of your four back-row zones, where they keep buffing your side. Click the next free socket to set one.

  5. Movement and combat

    Select one of your Vessels to see reachable tiles. Move, then attack anything in range. Adjacent defenders retaliate, so a bad attack costs you Guard too.

  6. Flux

    Flux pays for cards. It refills at the start of your turn and grows by one each turn, up to ten. Running out of Flux does not stop you moving or attacking.

  7. Ending a turn

    Press End Turn, or let the two-minute timer expire. Either way the turn advances exactly once and the opponent acts.

  8. Striking the Nexus

    Once the opponent controls no Vessels, move one of yours onto their home row and attack the Nexus directly. That is how matches end.

THE 65 OFFICIAL VESSELS

Five elements, sixty-five silhouettes

These are the same voxel models the battlefield renders. Each Vessel has its own proportions, gear and palette accent; shared material palettes keep the elements coherent. The scene behind each model is that Vessel's card illustration without the painted figure — one definition drives the diorama stage and the card art. Drag a model, or focus it and press the arrow keys, to inspect it from any angle.

THE PRINTED SET · 107 CARDS

The card face is the rulebook

These are the finished designs, one file per card, generated from the same card data the engine plays and the same voxel models the battlefield renders. The top-right medallion is always Flux cost; Attack and Guard are the printed combat values. Select any face to open it at full size.
