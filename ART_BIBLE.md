# Art Bible

## Identity
Stylized voxel-inspired 3D creatures on a tactical grid, paired with a crisp 2D interface. Favor distinct silhouettes, restrained effects, and readable board states.

## Element motifs
- Wood: bark, roots, growth rings, living fibers.
- Fire: ember cores, angular flame shapes, charred surfaces.
- Earth: stone strata, clay, mineral facets, weighty forms.
- Metal: forged plates, rivets, polished edges, mechanical precision.
- Water: glassy surfaces, fluid shapes, droplets, mist.

Do not rely on color alone to communicate element. Contrast and accessibility take precedence.

## Asset pipeline
Establish scale and camera, block out silhouettes, test them on the actual board, export glTF/GLB when ready, optimize for browser performance, and record asset provenance/licenses. Plan reusable animation families for humanoid, quadruped, flying, serpentine, and constructed forms. Minimum states: idle, summon, move, attack, hit, defeat. Do not assume every card needs bespoke animation.

Do not trace or closely imitate commercial-game models, card frames, UI, iconography, creatures, or effects. Build a coherent original Veylattice visual language.
