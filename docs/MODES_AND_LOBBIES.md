# Modes, AI and Online Lobbies

Status: requested mode scope; numbers identified as proposed defaults need playtesting.

## Solo
Player-owned legal deck against selectable AI personas, difficulty and deck combinations. Eligible server-validated matches earn currency; local demo matches do not. Proposed personas: Ember Vanguard (low-curve pressure), Mireglass Flanker (mobility/direct-lane threats), First Mountain Warden (defense/control), Lost Choir Keeper (revive/discard value), Gilt-Ash Schemer (resource denial/combo), Rootbound Mourner (fallen-allies/death growth). Each persona has at least two validated deck templates and substitution pools; vary deterministic seeds without violating ownership-free AI deck legality, cost or copy limits.

## Practice
Player chooses a prebuilt deck; opponent receives a randomized prebuilt deck. No currency, ranked progress, pack rewards or reward-odds credit. Free prebuilt access does not grant inventory ownership. Include tutorial hints, visible legal targets and optional explanations of AI moves.

## Ranked versus AI
Not a PvP ladder. Unranked -> Bronze -> Silver -> Gold, monthly seasons, no rank degradation during a season. Proposed promotion interpretation: each step uses a three-match preliminary series requiring two wins; failure restarts the series at the same current rank, never demotes. Draws/void matches do not fill a series slot. Display three progress pips and next-rank requirements. Gold remains Gold until monthly reset. Reset current rank/series on the first day of each UTC month; retain cards, currency, profiles and historical emblems.
Use fixed server-assigned AI difficulty bands and curated progressively stronger decks, with rotation among archetypes to discourage one counter-deck solution. Unranked uses tutorial/easy, Bronze standard, Silver hard, Gold expert. Better AI uses search/position evaluation, not hidden-hand access, free cards, favorable RNG or illegal resources. Award a permanent season-stamped emblem on first reaching each division. Old-season matches settle to their starting season and do not advance the new season; settlement must be idempotent.

## Tag 2v2
Four seats A1,B1,A2,B2; every seat independently supports human or AI. Permit 2 humans vs 2 AI, 2v2 humans, human+AI vs human+AI, human+AI vs 2 AI, and all other seat combinations including AI-only observation. At least one human participant is required for any reward; spectators never earn rewards. No shared hands/decks/Flux/discards. Each seat controls only its own units; ally-targeting effects explicitly identify allied versus owned targets.
Proposed defaults: shared 40 Vitality per team, 9x7 board, home deployment rows 0-2 versus 4-6, shared team Nexus and board, four Vessels/two Relics per seat, 120 seconds per seat turn, alternating A1 -> B1 -> A2 -> B2. Direct attacks require no opposing-team Vessels and presence on the opposing home row. Pool refresh/draw/summon allowance occur only on that seat's turn. A team loses at zero shared Vitality; simultaneous lethal draws. Shared-team buffs never stack accidentally due to duplicate seat ownership. These tag dimensions/life totals are provisional.

## Lobby UX and lifecycle
Create room -> show code/copyable invite -> join by code/link -> assign team/seat -> select legal deck or AI persona/difficulty -> all humans ready -> host requests start -> server locks configuration/validates decks -> match. Host can configure AI seats, privacy and mode before ready; a configuration/deck change clears readiness. Show full/not-found/expired/version-mismatch/reconnecting errors, connection badges, kick/leave confirmations and retry controls. Ready AI is explicit, not an invisible filler.
The host controls room setup, not match legality. Codes are generated with cryptographic randomness, collision-checked and rate-limited. Server enforces membership, seat occupancy and allowed configuration changes. Room state machine: waiting, ready, starting, in_match, finished, closed; transitions atomic. No joining a locked seat after start except its authenticated owner reconnecting.
On reconnect fetch an audience-filtered snapshot and resume from event sequence; reject stale/out-of-order commands. Proposed 60-second reconnect grace runs concurrently with turn timer, never pauses it. Casual tag may hand a disconnected seat to AI after grace; ranked solo disconnect follows timeout/forfeit. Returning owner reclaims an AI-controlled seat only at its next turn boundary. Explicit surrender forfeits the team after a teammate confirmation in tag (proposed policy). If host leaves lobby transfer host to earliest remaining human; if everyone leaves close after expiry. In-match authority remains server-owned regardless of lobby host.

## AI implementation and tests
AI enumerates the same legal commands as players and observes only its own hand plus public state. Score lethal/avoid-lethal first, then trades/retaliation risk, positioning, resource curve, deck identity and objective pressure. Easy uses shallow noisy scoring; standard one-ply evaluation; hard/expert bounded multi-ply/beam search with time/node limits. Always finish before deadline with a legal fallback/end turn. Team AI evaluates shared health, blocks allied lanes responsibly and coordinates without private opponent data. Test every seat composition, four-seat readiness, collisions, ownership, private hands, host departure, grace expiry, AI takeover and duplicate start requests.
