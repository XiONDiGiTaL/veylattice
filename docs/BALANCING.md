# Balancing Plan

Track win rate by starting player, element, and matchup; match duration; turn of first Vitality damage; Vessel lifespan; card play frequency; cards stranded in hand; Flux spent per turn; direct attacks per match; and frequency of full boards/stalls.

Change few variables per iteration, preserve match logs and card-set versions, test multiple play styles, and distinguish rules bugs from balance problems. Avoid conclusions from tiny samples. Rarity must not substitute for balance.
