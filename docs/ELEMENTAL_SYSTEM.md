# Elemental System

## Counter-cycle
- Wood overcomes Earth.
- Earth overcomes Water.
- Water overcomes Fire.
- Fire overcomes Metal.
- Metal overcomes Wood.

Only directly connected pairs receive a modifier; all other pairs are neutral unless a card says otherwise.

## Proposed combat modifiers
- If the defender's element overcomes the attacker, halve the attacker's Attack, rounded down, minimum effective Attack 1.
- If the attacker's element overcomes the defender, halve the defender's Guard, rounded down, minimum effective Guard 1.

Calculate from printed stats independently. Same-element and non-connected pairs are neutral. Centralize this logic, test all 25 pairs, and show the reason for modified stats in the UI. Treat this mechanic as provisional until playtested.
